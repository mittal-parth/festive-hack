How to play

Choose a cover to play. Use WASD or the arrow keys to walk freely. You can also click the ground to move there.

Follow the objective in the scene. Work with the objects around you to finish your family’s preparations, then take control of Rama in Ayodhya.

Use Worlds above the game to return to the arcade. Reopening a world starts a fresh playthrough.
