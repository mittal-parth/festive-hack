How to play

Choose a cover to enter its world. Walk with the arrow keys or WASD, or tap a spot to move there.

Follow the task shown in the scene. Each family’s preparations lead into a playable telling of Rama, Sita and Lakshmana’s homecoming in Ayodhya.

Use Worlds above the game to return to the arcade. Reopening a world starts a fresh playthrough.
